## there are the six advantages of the cloud computing:
### Trade capital expense for variable expense are the two:
*1.CapEx and OpEx *  *the CapEx is the physical server which is to be mainted and infrastructure and maintanence*
*the OpEX is the pay-as-you-go service modeling and which is used by the pay only for the usage of the server*
*2.Benefit from massive economies of scale — AWS aggregates usage across huge numbers of
customers, driving per-unit costs down, passed on to you.
3. Stop guessing capacity — no more over-provisioning “just in case” or under-provisioning and
running out; scale to actual demand.
4. Increase speed and agility — new resources available in minutes, not weeks of procurement.
5. Stop spending money running and maintaining data centers — focus on what differentiates
your business, not racking servers.
6. Go global in minutes — deploy in multiple AWS Regions worldwide with a few clicks.*
